# Board-Man MIT and Brand Boundary

After the independent-core migration gate is complete, Board-Man client source code is intended to be distributed under the MIT License.

The MIT License permits use, copying, modification, distribution, sublicensing, sale, and commercial use of the covered software subject to the license notice requirements.

## Brand is separate

The software license does not grant a separate right to represent a modified build as an official Board-Man release or official UniPlanck service.

The following are treated separately from the MIT software license:

- Board-Man product name as an official product identifier
- Board-Man logo and artwork
- UniPlanck name and logo
- official website identity
- official signing identities and notarized releases
- official hosted commercial services

Third-party forks should use their own product identity when their distribution could reasonably be confused with an official Board-Man release.

Access to hosted Board-Man services is governed by the service terms and entitlement issued by the private commercial service. MIT rights to client source code do not imply free access to hosted infrastructure, paid APIs, AI provider usage, cloud storage, team services, or support.
